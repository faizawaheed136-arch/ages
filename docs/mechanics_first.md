# AGES: mechanics are the priority, from now on

Standing direction given 2026-07-31, unprompted, after reviewing the work system:

> "there should be a clear way to sign up for a job like arrows leading u to it or
> something as this is really vague and when working there should be good mechanics
> to do stuff to keep one busy, thats what will make the game pop, good mechanics so
> focus on that too from now on pls"

## What this means for every future part

**Systems are not features until you can feel them with your hands.** A number that
goes up on a HUD is not a mechanic. Standing, money, stats — the simulation layer is
in decent shape. The thing that is missing, and the thing to bias every future
decision toward, is *what the player is physically doing minute to minute*.

Two specific complaints behind it, both fair:

1. **Discovery is vague.** Nothing in the world tells you a job exists, where it is,
   or that you qualify. Getting hired happened silently by walking within a few studs
   of an untagged-looking part. The player asked for *arrows leading you to it* — the
   literal ask is signposting and guidance, the real ask is that intent should never
   be a guess.
2. **Doing the thing is empty.** A shift was four real minutes of standing still while
   a wage ticked, punctuated by one text prompt. Correct as a simulation, dead as a
   game.

## How to apply it

- When speccing anything, lead with the verb — what does the player *do*? If the
  honest answer is "waits" or "reads a number", the design is not finished.
- Prefer mechanics that reuse the world that already exists (markers, NPCs, places)
  over new UI panels. This game's thesis is "the world is the menu"; a mechanic that
  resolves into a screen is fighting the thesis.
- Idle time inside an activity is a bug. If a system has a duration, it needs
  something to fill the duration.
- Signposting is part of the mechanic, not polish added afterward. A system the
  player cannot find does not exist.

Do not treat this as a one-off request satisfied by one build. It is a change to the
weighting of every future call.
