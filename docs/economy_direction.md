**Game currency lands with the world (Build Order step 3), not before.** It must
be earnable throughout a life and spent on things that make the game fun — a
faucet and a sink shipping in the same step. Step 2 events stay stat-only and do
not pay out money.

**Paid revives are off the table.** Rejected 2026-07-28 after I argued against
them and the user agreed to defer the whole question. Death is the core loop in
AGES, not a fail state, so selling an escape sells a way out of the thing the
game is about. The decisive argument: once revives are purchasable, every
mortality number becomes a revenue decision and death rates can never be tuned
honestly again.

If second chances are ever wanted, the agreed shape is diegetic and paid in soft
currency — expensive, rare, thematic — with real-money monetization aimed at
breadth instead (appearance, eras, extra concurrent lives).

**Why:** the user's stated ambition is for AGES to be the best game, full stop,
and the standard Roblox revive-gamepass move directly undercuts that by
corrupting difficulty tuning.

**How to apply:** don't propose paid revives again. When currency work starts,
build it server-authoritative with explicit purchase-receipt failure handling
from the first line — retrofitting that onto a live economy is where exploits
live. Monetization *content* comes last, after the loop is proven fun; the
transaction *plumbing* comes with the currency itself.
